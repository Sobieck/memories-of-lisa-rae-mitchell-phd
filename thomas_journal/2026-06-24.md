I wrote this stuff before I went to bed last night:

"I remember the last time I put Lisa to bed. She was having a hard time getting up the stairs. Probably because she was overwhelmed with what she was going to do in retrospect. So I had to help her get up the stairs but guiding her with my hands on her shoulders. I told her it was so nice to touch her.

I miss her every day. She was just everything to me.

I think one thing I appreciate about the whole experience is that I learned I was the sort of person that would do almost anything for a loved one in distress. I don’t think you know that sort of thing until you are confronted by terrible circumstances. I wish I didn’t know this about myself because of what it means in terms of what I've experienced. But it has brought me a great sense of accomplishment that I did what I did. I’m proud of myself. But fuck do I miss her."
