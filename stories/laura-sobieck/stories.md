I have put off sitting down to write about my beautiful sister-in-law Lisa because I just did not want to think about why she is no longer with us. I had not had a personal conversation with her in a decade, but I still thought of her often and am incredibly heartbroken about the way life sometimes leads us and seems to let us down. I included pictures that make me smile and remind me of the best times that I had with her.

I remember meeting Lisa in the midst of the chaos that is a Sobieck family function. I believe we were celebrating a late spring birthday or birthdays and Tom brought Lisa to Mary Claire’s house. I’m sure we completely overwhelmed her (very legitimately) once we put all the little kids in the kiddie pool and washed their hair using the garden hose. If she didn’t want to come to another function, I wouldn’t have blamed her at all. Despite the chaos, she returned to gathering after gathering and was always so kind and gentle with the children even though I have no doubt that it was a lot for her all at once. It  probably didn’t hurt that she drank wine along with the rest of us!

I always appreciated Lisa’s positive outlook and wonderful smile. She had a fantastic disposition and we had the best time together whether it was with all of the family or individually. Or shopping with MC and letting her talk us into buying clothes we didn’t need, lol. I appreciated that Tom found someone my age so she and I could talk about all of the things we enjoyed growing up. Despite the difference between Southern California and Oklahoma, we really did have a lot of junior high, high school, and college experiences that were similar. I always enjoyed talking to her about her work since I had a counseling background. It made me feel like I was honing my knowledge at a time when I was used to only talking to people under the age of 6. Lisa was so knowledgeable in her field and the VA hospital here was lucky to have someone who cared so much on staff. She came along at a time when I needed an adult friend and I was glad to have her.

I was honored to host her bridal shower and be the photographer at her and Tom’s wedding. It really was a “My Big Fat Greek Wedding” situation even though it was only immediate family in attendance. I remember we were all joking about it at the time, but it really was a perfect wedding. Lisa looked so beautiful and happy that day and Tom seemed overjoyed. I am so glad that I got to be a part of it. It wasn’t too long after that I was no longer involved in the family events. That day is one I hold in my heart.

I really wish I had kept in touch with her better throughout the years. I hope she knew I was thinking about her and Tom and appreciated them and what they were going through. The way life deals cards is often underhanded and callous. There was not a better person on earth than Lisa Mitchell and I am glad to have shared just a portion of her life.
