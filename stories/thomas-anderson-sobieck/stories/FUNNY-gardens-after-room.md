Lisa loved her gardens and she took enormous amounts of pride in making a pretty front yard with lots of flowers. She would spend hours weeding and planting when she was relatively healthy. The dystonia first took this from her. She just couldn’t do it anymore without pain so we had to hire someone and she always missed it. 

But the first time she was in the dark room for six months I was working, trying to take care of her, and generally overwhelmed. 

When we finally got her out of that it was so joyous but she was so upset about the state of her gardens. She just couldn’t get over that I deprioritized that. I think we got into an argument about it and I was just exasperated by it. It just didn’t matter to me with everything else. I thought it was funny to be honest.
