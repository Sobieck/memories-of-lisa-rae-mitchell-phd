When we went to the APA convention in Orlando we stayed at the peabody hotel. 

This is beside the point, but there was some tension that trip. Lisa was upset with me because of my lack of direction in my career at that point. I had stopped with my attempts to invest as a career largely because Lisa was unsupportive of it. I just don't think she understood what I was doing and she wanted me to have a normal job. But I remember having an argument with her while we had dinner one time on that trip about that.  

But the main thing we took away from this trip is that she just fucking loved that coffee at the hotel. It was really good and she just enjoyed it so. 

The coffee she made was from a little drip coffee maker with folgers or something so she wasn't having the best coffee experience at home. So I decided to learn about coffee and start making her a nice cup every morning. I always woke up earlier than her so it was a simple little thing for me to do for her. 

So I researched beans, got a grinder, and got a chemex. I would get the water at the exact temp and then brew the coffee. I did this for years. I would wake her up and she would have a perfect cup of coffee. I don't know why this is making me cry. But I just loved her so much. 

A few times I made little coffee cakes from Thomas Keller's cookbook and I would bring them up to her as well on the weekend so she could have a nice little breakfast in bed.
