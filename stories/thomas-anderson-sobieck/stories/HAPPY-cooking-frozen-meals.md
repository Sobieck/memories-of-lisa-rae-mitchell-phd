When I was at midway Lisa would cook big batches of burritos and meat pies from the cookbook "The make ahead cook" from america's test kitchen (I think). 

She'd heat up the hand pies and give them to me on the weekends while I watched manchester united. They were incredible. The crusts were homemade and they had veggies and ground meat in them. 

I think the burritos had chicken in them and rice, beans, cilantro, etc. I'd just eat them whenever I was hungry. I can't remember if those were just snacks or something we would get out of the freezer to eat for dinners. But they were the best frozen burritos I've ever had. 

She just loved cooking, and I think she liked cooking for me because I'm so enthusiastic about food.
