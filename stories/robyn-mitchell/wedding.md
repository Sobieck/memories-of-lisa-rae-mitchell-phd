During Thomas and Lisa’s wedding Ray asked “Who gives this woman to be married to this man?” and he answered “Robyn and I do.” He then commented that in this modern era of equality one should also ask “Who gives this man to be married to this woman?”. Immediately the whole family said “We do!”

Gayle also talked about Thomas’ christening bonnet which, according to a tradition she spoke of, was to be kept and turned into a lapel pocket handkerchief at his wedding, which he did wear.

Toward the end of the celebratory meal Thomas’ grandfather Anderson presented Thomas, as the last grandson, with the tie he had worn at his own wedding many years before.
